About Eleuteria

Eleuteria is an independent coordinator participating in the RoboSats peer-to-peer Bitcoin trading network.

The coordinator provides neutral infrastructure for Bitcoin exchanges settled over Lightning.

No custodial user balances. No traditional accounts.
