Eleuteria is an independent coordinator participating in the RoboSats peer-to-peer Bitcoin trading network.

RoboSats enables people to exchange Bitcoin over Lightning without traditional accounts, custodial balances or identity verification.

Eleuteria exists to provide another independent point of coordination within that network.
